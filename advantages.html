Christmas Auction

Advantages in detail

Advantages are bought at auction or found hidden around the party. Some are sealed in envelopes, so you may not know what you have until you open it.

AdvantageWhat it does
Wet Bandits PlotCollect $5 from every other player.
You Shot Your Eye Out!The next round, someone of your choosing has to turn around and can’t hear what’s being bid on.
Clark’s Christmas BonusYour bid on one gift counts double against other bids, but you pay only what you bid. Announce it as you bid.
The Grinch’s SackSteal a gift from another player’s pile. Name your target and the gift you want. A stolen gift is locked and can’t be stolen again.
Kevin’s Booby TrapProtects your pile from a Steal. Keep it secret until someone targets you, then reveal it to waste their Steal.
Naughty ListPick one player. They can’t bid on the gift currently up for auction.
Ralphie’s Decoder PinPeek inside one hidden gift before you bid. Keep what you saw to yourself.
Bailey’s BailoutTake $10 from the bank. It’s a wonderful life.
