Christmas Auction

Advantages in detail

Advantages are bought at auction or found hidden around the party. Some are sealed in envelopes, so you may not know what you have until you open it. Reveal them as you plan to use them during the auction.

AdvantageWhat it does
You'll Shoot Your Eye Out!The next round, someone of your choosing has to turn around and can’t hear what’s being bid on.
Clark’s Christmas BonusYour starting bid on one gift counts as double, but you pay only what you bid. (The advantage goes away if someone outbids it.)
Grinch's Santa SackSwap one of your gifts with a gift from another player’s gifts. Name your target and the gift you want.
Kevin’s Booby TrapProtects you from a player's Santa's Naughty List, You'll Shoot Your Eye Out! and Grinch's Santa Sack. You then can use their effect on them next round.
Santa's Naughty ListPick one player. They can’t add any additional bids on the gift currently up for auction. (Existing bids are unaffected.)
Bailey’s BailoutCollect 10% from the bank.
Ghost of Christmas FutureAnnounce you're using this advantage and write down who you think will win the next gift (besides yourself.) If you're right, you can pick any unauctioned gift to have.
