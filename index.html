Christmas Auction

Rules

  1. Bring gifts: Any number of gifts, one big or many small, wrapped or unwrapped, with a total amount adding up to your group’s amount.
  2. Bid: Everyone starts with the same amount of auction money and bids for the gifts they want.
  3. Score advantages: Bonuses that help during the auction are sold at auction and also hidden throughout the party.
  4. Play games: Win extra auction money by playing games like Saran Wrap Ball and Christmas Trivia.
  5. Auctioneer: Designate a party member to be the auctioneer. They can bid alongside auctioneering or designate someone to bid for them.
